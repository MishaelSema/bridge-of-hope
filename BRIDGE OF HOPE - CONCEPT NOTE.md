# **BRIDGE OF HOPE**

## **Project Title** 

## **Inclusive Rehabilitation and Skills Development for Persons with Disabilities in Cameroon** 

## **Background / Problem Statement** 

Persons with disabilities in Cameroon face significant barriers to accessing essential services such as healthcare, education, and employment. In rural and underserved communities, access to rehabilitation services is extremely limited, leading to increased dependency and s ocial exclusion. 

Additionally, many persons with disabilities lack access to vocational training and economic o pportunities, which further deepens poverty and marginalization. Social stigma and lack of awareness also hinder their full participation in society. 

There is therefore a critical need for integrated interventions that combine rehabilitation, skills development, and community inclusion. 

## **Project Goal** 

To improve the quality of life and socio-economic inclusion of persons with disabilities in Cameroon. 

## **Specific Objectives** 

1. To provide accessible rehabilitation services to persons with disabilities 

2. To equip beneficiaries with vocational and entrepreneurial skills 

3. To promote social inclusion and reduce stigma through community awareness 

4. To enhance income-generating opportunities for beneficiaries 

## **Target Beneficiaries** 

- 1000 persons with disabilities (youth and women prioritized) 

- Families and caregivers 

- Local communities in and around Yaoundé and Ekombitié

## ABOUT THE FOUNDER 

## **EKOM CHRISTOPHER ANYANG** 

## **Founder & Visionary Leader, Bridge of Hope Foundation** 

Ekom Christopher Anyang is a seasoned executive, international business professional, and development advocate with more than two decades of experience working at the intersection of international trade, investment, business development, economic diplomacy, and institutional engagement. 

He brings a distinctive combination of strategic leadership, international market knowledge, entrepreneurship, stakeholder engagement, and a deep commitment to human potential and community development. 

During his professional career, Christopher served for more than 22 years in commercial diplomacy at the U.S. Embassy in Cameroon, where he worked to strengthen trade and investment relationships between the United States and Cameroon. His work involved supporting companies seeking to enter or expand in international markets, connecting businesses and investors with potential partners, promoting investment opportunities, and engaging government, private-sector, and institutional stakeholders. 

His experience has given him a practical understanding of how business, investment, skills, partnerships, and access to opportunity can transform individuals and communities. 

It is this experience that informs his vision for the Bridge of Hope Foundation.
