## **1. Executive Summary** 

## **Mission:** 

To empower persons with disabilities in Yaoundé and Cameroon at large, through rehabilitation, advocacy, vocational and technical training, and community integration programs, enabling independence, economic self-reliance, and full social inclusion. 

## **Vision:** 

A Cameroon where persons with disabilities are fully included, respected, and equipped with skills and opportunities to thrive. 

## **Objectives:** 

- Deliver comprehensive rehabilitation services for physical, sensory, and intellectual disabilities. 

- Provide technical and vocational training aligned with Cameroon's labor market needs. 

- Promote disability empowerment initiatives, including leadership, entrepreneurship, and advocacy. 

- Influence policy and public perception on disability rights and inclusion.
