## Stack

Next.js (App Router) + MongoDB + Cloudinary (image storage) + SMTP (form email notifications). Chosen by the user directly, not delegated.

## Users

1. **Donors, grant-making foundations and international NGOs** — evaluating whether Bridge of Hope is credible enough to fund an ask of ~600 million XAF (≈ USD $1M) for setup plus first two years.
2. **Partners** — Cameroonian authorities (MINATD, Ministry of Social Affairs, MINSANTE), hospitals, schools, local businesses, and the sibling ACE entities.
3. **Persons with disabilities and their families/caregivers** in Yaoundé and rural communities (incl. Ekombitié village, ~70 km from Yaoundé) — trying to find out what services exist and how to reach them.
4. **The general public** — audience for anti-stigma advocacy and awareness.

## Product Purpose

The official public presence of **BRIDGE OF HOPE FOUNDATION**, a disability-inclusion NGO in Cameroon. It must (a) establish institutional credibility for donors and partners, (b) explain the programme model and its philosophy, (c) give beneficiaries and families a clear route to services, and (d) capture contact/pledge intent. Success = a first-time visitor understands what Bridge of Hope is, why it matters, and what to do within seconds — and a donor leaves convinced the organisation is a credible steward of funds.

## Positioning

Bridge of Hope combines **rehabilitation + vocational/technical training + advocacy + economic empowerment** in one integrated programme — the gap it names in the market is that existing Cameroonian centres are urban-focused and do not pair rehabilitation with income-generating skills. Its mechanism is *connection*: people↔knowledge, communities↔resources, entrepreneurs↔markets, potential↔opportunity. Stated commitment: social impact must move **beyond assistance** toward capability, enterprise, employment and independence.

## Product Principles

1. **Dignity is the product.** Income, skills and therapy are instruments; the outcome is a person participating with dignity in economic and social life. Nothing in the site may frame beneficiaries as objects of pity.
2. **Connection is the mechanism.** Every section should show a bridge between two things that already exist but are not linked.
3. **Business discipline serves the mission.** Credibility with donors comes from institutions, KPIs and clear asks — not sentiment.
4. **Beyond assistance.** The through-line from rehabilitation to skills to markets must be visible as one pathway, not four disconnected services.
5. **Show, don't claim.** Where numbers or imagery do not exist yet, mark the gap plainly rather than inventing proof.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

**WCAG 2.2 AA is a brand requirement, not a checkbox** — a disability-inclusion organisation shipping an inaccessible site undermines its own mission. Required from day one: keyboard navigability, visible focus, contrast ≥ 4.5:1, semantic heading structure, text scaling to 200%+ with no text-in-images, captions/alt text discipline, plain reading level on beneficiary-facing copy, and `prefers-reduced-motion` respected. Stated visibly in the site.
