# Surface brief — bridge-of-hope/site (primary target: site)

Scope: the whole public website, first surface = Home. Visitor mode: **Persuade** (donors, partners, authorities and families all arrive to decide whether this foundation is credible and what to do about it).

Audience: international donors/funders (evaluating a ~600M XAF ask), Cameroonian partners and authorities, persons with disabilities and their families, and the general public. Job: understand what Bridge of Hope is, why it matters, and act — pledge/partner, or find services. Proof: the programme model itself (rehabilitation → skills → markets → dignity), the founder's 22 years of commercial diplomacy, and the institutional record (governance, KPIs, legal registration). Constraints: bilingual EN/FR, WCAG 2.2 AA as a brand requirement, no fabricated numbers, imagery is placeholder until real photography arrives, contact/pledge form only (no payments).

## Direction contract

THESIS: Bridge of Hope's offer is an integrated pathway from rehabilitation to economic independence, and the category default — hopeful hero photo, three equal programme cards, a blue Donate button — buries it. This page refuses that arrangement: it is a **two-page editorial spread**, where the claim poses on the left and its evidence answers on the right, and the gutter holds the beat. The programme model is demonstrated across the gutter instead of listed in a card row, and the ask arrives as the closing spread, not a button in a hero.

OWN-WORLD: warm white paper ground `#F6F1E7`, charcoal photogravure ink `#1C1A17`, photographic greys `#8F8B83`, and exactly one live element in lipstick red `#A32127`. Type is a didone at display size only — **Bodoni Moda**, hairline contrast, negative tracking, tight leading — against **Jost** for text, captions and controls (the historic Didot/Futura pairing). Components: full-bleed photographic plates that bleed to the gutter, ragged-right sparse text set around images, hairline rules as the only structural device, captions as small ranked lines, and the red reserved for the one action and the one urgent figure. No cards, no shadows, no rounded corners, no icon tiles.

STORY: the visitor arrives on a full-bleed plate and one didot line, learns in three spreads that this is a Cameroonian foundation running one continuous pathway (rehabilitation → skills → markets → dignity), meets the founder and the institution behind it, sees the honest state of the record (targets and estimates marked as such, provenance on every figure), and is left with one action in red — support the work, partner, or find services.

FIRST VIEWPORT: a photogravure plate filling the left page and bleeding into the gutter; on the right page, set large and ragged, the foundation name in Bodoni Moda over one line of mission; a single ranked caption line beneath the plate; and the one red element — the primary action — sitting alone in the lower right of the right page. Nothing else. The section below the fold begins as a spread, not as more hero.

FORM: a two-page editorial spread, the Brodovitch Harper's Bazaar world, seed key `de213184`, won by the user over the assigned ledger direction (my own top-ranked candidate was the joined-strip Ndop cloth) and locked as challenger `challenger-bazaar`. Memorable moment: the gutter — the moment a sentence posed on the left page is answered by its evidence on the right, with the red action waiting at the foot of the turn.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.

Unresolved decisions: donation provider, domain, real photography and logo, verified impact numbers, and the founder's canonical name form.
